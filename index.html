Help for Awayline.

Awayline brings your herdr sessions to iPhone and iPad: check on agents, answer a question, and work in the terminal on your own machine.

Make your first connection

Start with the Mac and Linux setup guide. You’ll need SSH access and a route to your machine. Awayline can set up herdr when you connect.

When a connection needs attention

Read connection troubleshooting and background-alert limits, or email linyoutian.loyot@gmail.com.

Include your app version, device OS, and the steps that led to the problem. Help → Review diagnostics in Awayline produces a report you can inspect before sharing. Keep private keys, passwords, and sensitive terminal output out of support messages.

Your data stays under your control

Awayline connects to machines you choose. Read the privacy policy for credential storage, attachments, dictation, notifications, and support correspondence.
